## Methodology

1. **Reconnaissance** — Nmap port/service enumeration.
2. **Automated scanning** — OWASP ZAP spider, passive scan, and (for
   `full`-profile targets only) active scan.
3. **Manual testing** — Burp Suite findings exported and imported.
4. **Exploitation confirmation** — select findings manually verified in an
   isolated lab environment (see Manual PoC Notes below); SAINT does not
   automate exploitation.
5. **Aggregation** — findings deduplicated, severity-normalized, and mapped
   to OWASP Top 10 categories.
